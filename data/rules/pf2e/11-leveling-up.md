Characters gain a level every 1,000 XP, from 1st up to 20th.

## Gaining a level

1. Add 1 to your level and subtract **1,000 XP**; anything left over counts toward the next level.
2. Raise your maximum HP by your **class's HP per level** (the class number plus your Constitution modifier).
3. Take everything on your class table's row for the new level: class features, feats, skill increases, attribute boosts, spells and slots.
4. Every trained-or-better proficiency bonus goes up by **1**, since it includes your level: AC, attacks, Perception, saves, skills, spell DC and class DC all rise.
5. Update anything based on your level or changed attributes. A higher Constitution adds 1 HP per level; a higher Intelligence adds a trained skill and a language.

You can do these in any order, so a boost taken this level can help you qualify for a feat taken this level.

_Player Core p. 29._

## What most classes gain

The Player Core classes follow this pattern, with their own features added:

| Levels                     | Gain                                                               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1                          | Ancestry and background, boosts, initial proficiencies, class feat |
| 2, 4, 6 … 20 (even levels) | Class feat and skill feat                                          |
| 3, 7, 11, 15, 19           | General feat                                                       |
| 3, 5, 7 … 19 (odd levels)  | Skill increase                                                     |
| 5, 9, 13, 17               | Ancestry feat                                                      |
| 5, 10, 15, 20              | Four attribute boosts                                              |

Rogues get skill feats and skill increases more often. Some classes gain a class feat at 1st level and others first at 2nd.

_Player Core p. 92._

## Skill increases and boosts

- A **skill increase** makes you trained in a new skill, or raises a trained skill to expert. From **7th level** it can raise expert to **master**; from **15th level**, master to **legendary**.
- At 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th level you boost **four different** attributes. A boost to a modifier of +4 or more is a partial boost: two of them make +1.

_Player Core pp. 19, 92._

## Retraining

In downtime you can change earlier choices with a teacher's help:

- **A feat** for another of the same type: a week.
- **A skill increase** (lower one skill a step, raise another, no higher than the rank you traded away): a week.
- **A class feature choice**: a week for small ones (a spell in your repertoire), at least a month for big ones.
- You can't retrain your ancestry, heritage, background, class or attribute modifiers.

_Player Core pp. 440–441._
