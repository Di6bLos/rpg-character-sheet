Combat happens in **encounter mode**: rounds of about 6 seconds, in which everyone takes one turn in initiative order.

## Initiative

- The GM calls for initiative at the start of the encounter. You usually roll **Perception**, but the GM may ask for another check (Stealth if you were Avoiding Notice, Deception or Diplomacy in a social scene).
- Highest result goes first. **A PC tied with an enemy goes after the enemy**; tied PCs choose between themselves.
- Nobody rerolls: every round runs in the same order until the encounter ends. **Delay** moves your turn later; being knocked out moves it too.

_Player Core p. 435._

## Your turn

**Start of turn**, in any order: count down effects you made that last a number of rounds, use one free action or reaction triggered by your turn beginning, roll a recovery check if you're dying, and resolve anything else that happens now. Then, always last, **regain 3 actions and 1 reaction**. An unspent reaction from last round is lost; nothing carries over.

**Act**: use actions and activities in any order, one at a time. A 2- or 3-action activity must be finished on the same turn. You can end your turn early, losing the actions you didn't use.

**End of turn**: end effects that last until the end of your turn (an unsustained spell ends here), take persistent damage and roll its flat check, roll saves against ongoing afflictions, then let conditions like frightened tick down; you can use one free action or reaction triggered by your turn ending.

_Player Core pp. 435–436._

## Attack rolls

- **Melee**: d20 + Strength (or Dexterity with a finesse weapon) + proficiency + bonuses + penalties.
- **Ranged**: d20 + Dexterity + proficiency + bonuses + penalties.
- **Spell attack**: d20 + your spellcasting attribute + proficiency + bonuses + penalties.
- Compare the result to the target's **AC**.

_Player Core pp. 402–403._

## Multiple attack penalty

Every action with the attack trait (Strikes, spell attack rolls, Grapple, Shove, Trip…) counts. The penalty is set by the weapon you're using for that attack, not the earlier ones.

| Attack in a turn | Penalty | Agile weapon |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------------ |
| First            | none    | none         |
| Second           | –5      | –4           |
| Third and later  | –10     | –8           |

It applies only on your own turn, so a reaction like Reactive Strike doesn't take it or add to it.

_Player Core pp. 402, 418._

## Range

Within a ranged weapon's first **range increment** there's no penalty. Each increment beyond that is **–2**, out to six increments (–10 at the last).

_Player Core p. 403._

## Damage

- **Melee and unarmed**: weapon dice + Strength + bonuses + penalties.
- **Ranged**: weapon dice, + Strength for a thrown weapon or half Strength for a propulsive one.
- **Spells and bombs**: their dice + bonuses + penalties; no attribute unless the effect says so.
- Of each bonus type you use only the highest; untyped penalties add up. Penalties can't drop damage below **1**.
- **Critical hit**: a Strike that critically succeeds deals **double damage**. Benefits that only happen on a crit (like a fatal die) aren't doubled.
- Halving rounds down, to a minimum of 1.
- Then apply the target's **immunities, then weaknesses, then resistances**, and take what's left off its Hit Points.
- **Nonlethal**: attacking nonlethally with a lethal weapon (or lethally with a nonlethal one, like a fist) takes a –2 circumstance penalty.

_Player Core pp. 275, 406–408._

## Persistent damage

You don't take it straight away: you take it at the end of each of your turns, then roll a **DC 15 flat check** to end it. Help that makes sense (dousing flames) can give an extra flat check, at DC 10 for especially fitting help.

_Player Core pp. 406, 445._

## Cover

| Cover    | Bonus                                  | Can Hide? |
| -------- | -------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Lesser   | +1 circumstance to AC                  | No        |
| Standard | +2 circumstance to AC, Reflex, Stealth | Yes       |
| Greater  | +4 circumstance to AC, Reflex, Stealth | Yes       |

- Draw a line between the centres of the two spaces: through terrain or an object is standard cover, through a creature is lesser cover. A creature two or more sizes larger than both of you gives standard cover.
- **Take Cover** turns standard cover into greater cover.
- The Reflex and Stealth bonus applies against area effects and to Hide, Sneak and other ways of avoiding detection.

_Player Core p. 424._

## Flanking and off-guard

- A foe is **off-guard** (–2 circumstance penalty to AC) to melee attacks from creatures flanking it.
- To flank, you and an ally must be on **opposite sides** of the foe (a line between your centres crosses opposite sides or corners of its space), both able to act, both able to make a melee attack, and both with the foe in reach.

_Player Core p. 425._

## Hard-to-see targets

| Target is  | Before you roll                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Concealed  | DC 5 flat check or the attack has no effect                        |
| Hidden     | DC 11 flat check; you're off-guard to it                           |
| Undetected | Guess its square; the GM rolls the flat check and attack in secret |

A failed flat check still spends your actions and any spell slot or cost. **Seek** can make an undetected creature hidden. Invisible creatures are undetected to anyone relying on sight.

_Player Core p. 434._

## Reactive Strike

Some classes and creatures have **Reactive Strike**: when a creature in its reach uses a manipulate or move action, makes a ranged attack or leaves a square during a move action, it makes a melee Strike against it. A critical hit disrupts a manipulate action. Leaving squares in its reach triggers it at most once per move action, and **Step** doesn't trigger it at all.

_Player Core pp. 138, 421._
