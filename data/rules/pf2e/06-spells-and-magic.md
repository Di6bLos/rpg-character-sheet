Spells come from four traditions and run from cantrips up to 10th rank. Your class decides how you get them, how many you can cast a day and which attribute powers them.

## Traditions

| Tradition | Flavour                                     | Typical casters |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Arcane    | Logic and study; the broadest list          | Wizard          |
| Divine    | Faith in powers beyond the world            | Cleric          |
| Occult    | The strange, the unexplained, mind and soul | Bard            |
| Primal    | Instinct and the natural world              | Druid           |

When you cast a spell, it takes your tradition's trait, even if it came from another list. Magic that belongs to no tradition (most items) has the magical trait instead.

_Player Core p. 297._

## Spell slots and ranks

- A spell's **rank** runs from 1 to 10. Your class table says how many slots of each rank you have.
- **Prepared casters** (cleric, druid, witch, wizard) pick their spells for each slot during daily preparations. Each prepared spell is spent when cast; prepare it twice to cast it twice.
- **Spontaneous casters** (such as the bard) keep a repertoire and choose which spell to use a slot on as they cast.
- **Heightening**: casting from a higher-rank slot raises the spell to that rank. A spontaneous caster must know the spell at that rank (signature spells get around this).
- **Heightened (+N)** entries add their effect for every N ranks above the spell's lowest rank. A 3rd-rank _fireball_ deals 6d6; with "Heightened (+1) 2d6" it deals 8d6 at 4th rank, 10d6 at 5th.

_Player Core p. 297._

## Cantrips, focus spells and innate spells

- **Cantrips** cost no slot: cast them at will. They're always heightened to **half your level, rounded up**.
- **Focus spells** come from class features and feats, cost **1 Focus Point** each and heighten like cantrips. Your pool holds one point per focus spell you know, **3 at most**. It refills at daily preparations, and **Refocus** (10 minutes) regains 1 point.
- **Innate spells** come from ancestry or items, usually once a day, refreshed at daily preparations. They use Charisma unless the source says otherwise and don't count as being a spellcaster.

_Player Core p. 298._

## Casting a spell

- Casting takes the actions the spell lists, with gestures and incantations, so being unable to speak usually stops spellcasting.
- Some spells list a **cost** (consumed) or a **locus** (an object you need but keep).
- Spells that take a minute or more can't be cast in an encounter.
- A **disrupted** spell is lost: the slot, cost and actions are spent with no effect.
- You need **line of effect** to targets and areas, and must be able to perceive a target to target it.
- **Spellshape** actions must come right before the spell they change.

_Player Core pp. 299–302._

## Spell attacks and saving throws

- **Spell attack** = d20 + your spellcasting attribute + proficiency. It's an attack roll against AC and counts toward the multiple attack penalty.
- **Spell DC** = 10 + your spellcasting attribute + proficiency.
- A **basic saving throw** decides damage alone:

| Target's save    | Damage taken |
| ---------------- | ------------ |
| Critical success | None         |
| Success          | Half         |
| Failure          | Full         |
| Critical failure | Double       |

_Player Core pp. 302–303, 403–404._

## Durations

- A duration in rounds counts down at the **start of the caster's turn**.
- **Sustained** spells last until the end of your next turn unless you use the **Sustain** action that turn.
- Spells "until your next daily preparations" can be kept going by leaving their slot unprepared.
- **Dismiss** ends a dismissible spell early.
- A spell keeps going if its caster is incapacitated or dies.

_Player Core p. 302._

## Counteracting

To end a spell or effect (with _dispel magic_, for example), roll a counteract check: your spellcasting attribute + spellcasting proficiency, against the effect's DC. A spell's **counteract rank** is its rank; for anything else, halve its level and round up.

| Your result      | You counteract an effect whose rank is… |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Critical success | no more than 3 higher than yours        |
| Success          | no more than 1 higher than yours        |
| Failure          | lower than yours                        |
| Critical failure | nothing                                 |

_Player Core pp. 303, 431._
