Hit Points come back through rest, the Medicine skill and magic. Healing never takes you above your maximum HP.

## Hit Points

- Your **maximum HP** is your ancestry HP plus your class HP at 1st level, plus your class HP again at every level after that, plus anything else (Toughness, for example).
- Damage comes off your current HP one for one. You can't go below 0.
- **Temporary HP** are lost first. Only one source at a time: when you'd gain more, choose to keep the old amount and duration or take the new. Healing can't restore lost temporary HP.
- **Fast healing** and **regeneration** restore their amount at the start of each of your turns. While regeneration works, your dying value can't rise high enough to kill you; damage of a type it lists switches it off until the end of your next turn.

_Player Core p. 410._

## Resting and daily preparations

- **Once every 24 hours** you can rest (usually 8 hours). You regain **Constitution modifier (minimum 1) × your level** HP, and some conditions improve.
- **Sleeping in armor** leaves you fatigued. Going **more than 16 hours** without rest makes you fatigued until you rest 8 hours straight.
- **Daily preparations** take about an hour after resting, once a day:
  - spellcasters regain spell slots, and prepared casters choose their spells;
  - Focus Points and once-a-day abilities (magic item uses included) reset;
  - you put on armor and ready your gear;
  - you invest up to **10** worn magic items.
- **Long-term rest** in downtime: a full day and night of rest restores **Constitution modifier (minimum 1) × twice your level** HP.

_Player Core pp. 439–440._

## Treat Wounds (Medicine)

An exploration activity: **10 minutes** with a healer's toolkit, on one living creature (you can treat yourself). The patient is then immune to Treat Wounds for **1 hour**, counted from the start, so they can be treated once an hour.

| Your Medicine | DC  | Success | Critical success |
| ------------- | --- | ------- | ---------------- |
| Trained       | 15  | 2d8     | 4d8              |
| Expert        | 20  | 2d8+10  | 4d8+10           |
| Master        | 30  | 2d8+30  | 4d8+30           |
| Legendary     | 40  | 2d8+50  | 4d8+50           |

- The higher DCs are optional: at expert you may keep using DC 15.
- A success or critical success also **removes the wounded condition**.
- A critical failure deals **1d8** damage instead.
- Keep treating for a full hour after a success to **double** the HP regained.

_Player Core p. 242._

## First aid in a fight

**Administer First Aid** (2 actions, Medicine, usable untrained, needs a healer's toolkit) on an adjacent creature:

- **Stabilize** a dying creature at 0 HP: DC = 5 + its recovery check DC, usually **15 + its dying value**. On a success it loses dying (but stays unconscious); on a critical failure its dying value goes up by 1.
- **Stop Bleeding**: usually the DC of the effect that caused the bleed. A success gives the patient an extra flat check to end the bleed at the lowered DC of 10; a critical failure deals the bleed damage at once.

_Player Core p. 241._

## Other healing

- **Healing spells and items** restore HP up to your maximum. Healing a dying creature to 1 HP or more wakes it.
- **Bleed** damage ends on its own if you're healed to full HP.
- The **wounded** condition ends after a successful Treat Wounds, or once you're at full HP and rest for 10 minutes.

_Player Core pp. 407, 411, 445._
