Conditions change what a creature can do, from frightened or prone to the attitudes that measure how an NPC feels about you. A condition lasts until its duration ends, something removes it, or its own text ends it.

## How conditions work

- **Values.** Some conditions have a number (frightened 2, dying 1). It usually sets the size of the penalty, and the condition ends when the value reaches 0.
- **No doubling up.** You can have a condition only once. If you'd gain it again, keep the longer duration. With values, apply only the highest, but track each duration: slowed 2 for 1 round and slowed 1 for 6 rounds is slowed 2, then slowed 1.
- **Removing** a condition removes all of it, whatever its value.
- **Overriding.** Some conditions suppress others (blinded overrides dazzled). The suppressed one keeps counting down.
- **Gaining and losing actions.** Quickened, slowed and stunned change how many actions you regain at the start of your turn; gaining quickened or slowed during your turn doesn't change that turn.
