In an encounter you get **3 actions and 1 reaction** each turn. Everything you do is one of four kinds of action.

## Kinds of action

| Kind          | What it means                                                                                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single action | One action, done in a moment (Stride, Strike, Interact)                                                   |
| Activity      | Two or three actions spent together on your turn, or a longer exploration or downtime task                |
| Reaction      | Used when its trigger happens, on anyone's turn; you get 1 a round                                        |
| Free action   | Costs no actions. With a trigger it works like a reaction; without one, like a single action on your turn |

- Finish one action or activity before starting another. Reactions and triggered free actions can interrupt, though.
- An activity that's interrupted or disrupted loses every action you put into it.
- Only one action can respond to a given trigger.
- **Speaking** normally isn't an action.
- When you lose actions (slowed, stunned), you choose which to lose. When you **can't act**, you can't use any actions, reactions or free actions, or even speak.
- **Ready** (2 actions) turns one single action into a reaction with a trigger you choose. **Delay** moves your whole turn later in the round.

_Player Core pp. 398, 414–415._
