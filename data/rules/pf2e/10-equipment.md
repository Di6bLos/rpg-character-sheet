What you carry is measured in Bulk, what you wear sets your AC, and what you wield needs free hands.

## Money

| Coin          | Worth             |
| ------------- | ----------------- |
| Copper (cp)   | 1/10 sp           |
| Silver (sp)   | The standard coin |
| Gold (gp)     | 10 sp             |
| Platinum (pp) | 10 gp             |

A new 1st-level character has **15 gp**. You sell items for **half their Price**.

_Player Core pp. 25, 267, 441._

## Hands and items

- Items are **held** (in your hands), **worn** (sheaths, pouches, bandoliers) or **stowed** (in a backpack).
- **Interact** (1 action) draws, stows, swaps one item for another, picks up, passes an item to a willing creature, or changes your grip by adding a hand. Getting something out of a backpack means opening it too.
- **Release** (free) drops an item or takes a hand off it.
- Drinking a potion from your belt is an Interact to draw it, then an action to drink.

_Player Core pp. 267–268._

## Bulk

- You can carry **5 + Strength modifier** Bulk without trouble. More than that makes you **encumbered**: clumsy 1 and –10 feet to all your Speeds (not below 5 feet).
- You can't carry more than **10 + Strength modifier** Bulk.
- **L** (light) items: 10 of them make 1 Bulk; round fractions down. **—** (negligible) items don't count.
- **1,000 coins** are 1 Bulk.
- As a rough guide, 5 to 10 pounds is 1 Bulk. A longsword is 1, full plate is 4, a dagger is L.
- Dragging something counts it at half its Bulk, at about 50 feet a minute.

| Creature size | Bulk |
| ------------- | ---- |
| Tiny          | 1    |
| Small         | 3    |
| Medium        | 6    |
| Large         | 12   |
| Huge          | 24   |
| Gargantuan    | 48   |

_Player Core p. 269._

## Armor

**AC = 10 + Dexterity (up to the Dex cap) + proficiency for the armor's category + the armor's item bonus + other bonuses and penalties.**

- **Dex cap**: the most Dexterity you can add while wearing it.
- **Check penalty**: applies to Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks (not attacks). Meeting the armor's **Strength** value removes it.
- **Speed penalty**: applies to all your Speeds. Meeting the Strength value reduces it by 5 feet.
- **Putting on armor**: 1 minute for light, 5 minutes for medium or heavy. Taking any armor off: 1 minute.
- Worn armor counts its listed Bulk; carried, it's 1 more.
- Sleeping in armor leaves you fatigued.

_Player Core pp. 271, 439._

## Shields

- A shield helps only when you **Raise a Shield** (1 action): its bonus applies to AC as a circumstance bonus until your next turn starts.
- With the **Shield Block** reaction, the shield's **Hardness** reduces the damage; you and the shield each take what's left.
- A tower shield lets you Take Cover for +4.
- A shield that isn't built as a weapon can still be used for a **shield bash**, a martial weapon.

_Player Core p. 274._

## Broken items

Items have **Hardness**, **HP** and a **Broken Threshold (BT)**. Damage minus Hardness comes off the item's HP; at or below its BT it's broken, and at 0 HP it's destroyed. A broken item doesn't work or give bonuses, except armor: broken armor still gives its AC bonus but adds a status penalty of –1 (light), –2 (medium) or –3 (heavy).

_Player Core p. 269._

## Weapons at a glance

- **Proficiency** with the weapon's category (simple, martial, advanced) comes from your class.
- **Damage dice**: a striking rune adds dice of the same size. "Increase the die size" goes d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12, once at most.
- **Agile** weapons lower the multiple attack penalty (–4/–8). **Finesse** melee weapons can use Dexterity to attack.
- **Improvised weapons** are simple weapons with a –2 item penalty to attack.
- **Unarmed attacks** aren't weapons; effects for weapons don't apply to them unless they say so.

_Player Core pp. 275, 282, 406._
