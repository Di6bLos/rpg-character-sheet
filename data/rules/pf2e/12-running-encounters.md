For the GM: how to size a fight and award its XP. The budget assumes a party of four.

## Threat and XP budget

| Threat   | XP budget  | Each PC more or fewer |
| -------- | ---------- | --------------------- |
| Trivial  | 40 or less | 10 or less            |
| Low      | 60         | 20                    |
| Moderate | 80         | 20                    |
| Severe   | 120        | 30                    |
| Extreme  | 160        | 40                    |

- **Trivial**: no real chance of losing. **Low**: uses some resources. **Moderate**: a serious challenge. **Severe**: the hardest fight most groups can expect to win, and a character could die. **Extreme**: an even match; use it only if you're willing to risk the whole party.
- For a bigger party add the adjustment per extra character, for a smaller one subtract it. The XP the party earns stays the amount for four.
- Spend the extra on more enemies rather than tougher ones.

_GM Core p. 75._

## Creature XP

Pick creatures from 4 levels below to 4 levels above the party.

| Creature level  | XP  | Suggested role                           |
| --------------- | --- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Party level – 4 | 10  | Low-threat lackey                        |
| Party level – 3 | 15  | Low- or moderate-threat lackey           |
| Party level – 2 | 20  | Any lackey or standard creature          |
| Party level – 1 | 30  | Any standard creature                    |
| Party level     | 40  | Any standard creature or low-threat boss |
| Party level + 1 | 60  | Low- or moderate-threat boss             |
| Party level + 2 | 80  | Moderate- or severe-threat boss          |
| Party level + 3 | 120 | Severe- or extreme-threat boss           |
| Party level + 4 | 160 | Extreme-threat solo boss                 |

_GM Core p. 76._
