Player characters (and important foes the GM chooses) don't die at 0 HP. They're knocked out and start dying, and the dying value decides what happens next.

## Knocked out

When you drop to 0 HP:

- **Your initiative moves** to just before the turn of the creature or effect that knocked you out.
- You gain **dying 1**, or **dying 2** if it was an attacker's critical success or your own critical failure.
- If you're **wounded**, add your wounded value to that dying value.
- From a **nonlethal** attack or effect you don't gain dying: you're unconscious at 0 HP instead.
- Most other creatures simply die at 0 HP; undead and constructs are destroyed.

_Player Core pp. 410, 442._

## Dying

- While dying you're **unconscious**. At **dying 4** you die.
- **Taking damage** while dying raises the value by **1**, or by **2** from an attacker's critical hit or your own critical failure.
- **Healing** to 1 HP or more ends dying and unconsciousness; you can act normally on your next turn.

_Player Core p. 411._

## Recovery checks

At the start of each of your turns while dying, roll a **flat check against DC 10 + your current dying value**.

| Result           | Dying value |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| Critical success | –2          |
| Success          | –1          |
| Failure          | +1          |
| Critical failure | +2          |

At dying 0 the condition ends. If you're still at 0 HP you stay unconscious until you wake up as the unconscious condition describes.

_Player Core p. 411._

## Wounded and doomed

- **Wounded**: every time you lose the dying condition you gain **wounded 1**, or your wounded value goes up by 1. It's added to dying the next time you go down. It ends after a successful **Treat Wounds**, or once you're at full HP and rest for 10 minutes.
- **Doomed**: lowers the dying value that kills you by its value (doomed 1 means you die at dying 3). If that limit ever reaches 0 you die. Doomed goes down by 1 after each full night's rest.

_Player Core p. 411._

## Heroic recovery

If you have at least 1 Hero Point, you can spend **all** of them at the start of your turn or when your dying value would increase. You lose dying entirely and stabilize at 0 HP, **without** gaining or raising wounded (you keep any wounded you already had).

_Player Core pp. 411, 413._

## Unconscious

You can't act, take a **–4 status penalty** to AC, Perception and Reflex saves, and are **blinded** and **off-guard**. You fall prone and drop what you're holding. At 0 HP but not dying, you wake at 1 HP after 10 minutes or more (the GM decides), or sooner if healed.

_Player Core pp. 411, 446._

## Death

- **Massive damage**: damage equal to or greater than **double your maximum HP** in one blow kills you outright.
- **Death effects** (the death trait) kill you if they bring you to 0 HP, without dying 4; some kill outright.
- A dead creature can't act and counts as an object; it stays at 0 HP until something like _raise dead_ or the _resurrect_ ritual brings it back.

_Player Core pp. 411–412._
