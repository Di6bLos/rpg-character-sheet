On a grid each square is 5 feet. Outside combat, movement is measured in travel speed and the party picks exploration activities instead of counting actions.

## Speed and moving

- **Stride** moves you up to your Speed (your land Speed). **Step** moves 5 feet without triggering reactions, but not into difficult terrain.
- **Diagonals**: the first diagonal square in a turn costs 5 feet, the second 10, and so on alternating. The count resets at the end of your turn.
- **Burrow, climb, fly and swim Speeds** need their own actions (Burrow, Climb, Fly, Swim). A climb or swim Speed means you succeed automatically and gives +4 circumstance to those Athletics checks.
- Penalties can't take a Speed below **5 feet**.
- Moving out of a square in an enemy's reach can trigger reactions, at most once per move action for each reacting creature.

_Player Core pp. 420–421._

## Terrain

- **Difficult terrain**: +5 feet to move into each square. **Greater difficult terrain**: +10 feet. Diagonals don't add more on top.
- Abilities that ignore difficult terrain let you move through greater difficult terrain as if it were ordinary difficult terrain.
- **Hazardous terrain** damages you as you move through it.
- **Narrow surfaces** and **uneven ground** need Balance, and make you off-guard. **Inclines** need Climb.

_Player Core p. 423._

## Size, space and reach

| Size       | Space            | Reach (tall) | Reach (long) |
| ---------- | ---------------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Tiny       | Less than 5 feet | 0 feet       | 0 feet       |
| Small      | 5 feet           | 5 feet       | 5 feet       |
| Medium     | 5 feet           | 5 feet       | 5 feet       |
| Large      | 10 feet          | 10 feet      | 5 feet       |
| Huge       | 15 feet          | 15 feet      | 10 feet      |
| Gargantuan | 20 feet or more  | 20 feet      | 15 feet      |

- You can move through a **willing** creature's space; an unwilling one needs **Tumble Through**. You can't end your turn in another creature's space.
- You can move through a creature three or more sizes larger or smaller than you. Tiny creatures can share spaces.

_Player Core pp. 421–422._

## Falling and forced movement

- A fall of more than 5 feet deals **bludgeoning damage equal to half the distance**, up to 750 at 1,500 feet, and you land prone if you take any. Water, snow and the like make it count as 20 feet shorter (30 if you dove).
- **Grab an Edge** or **Arrest a Fall** can reduce or stop a fall.
- **Forced movement** (Shove, Reposition) is set by the effect, not your Speed, and doesn't trigger movement reactions.

_Player Core pp. 421–422._

## Detection and light

| Lighting | Effect                                                                              |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bright   | You see normally                                                                    |
| Dim      | Creatures and objects are concealed, unless you have low-light vision or darkvision |
| Darkness | They're hidden or undetected, and you're blinded, unless you have darkvision        |

A light source lists the radius of its bright light and sheds dim light to double that radius.

Whether a creature is **observed**, **hidden**, **undetected** or **unnoticed** is tracked for each viewer. Use **Seek** to find hidden or undetected creatures; Stealth's **Hide** and **Sneak** keep you out of sight.

_Player Core pp. 432–434._

## Exploration activities

Tell the GM what you're doing as you travel; they match it to an activity. Most of them halve your travel speed.

| Activity          | What it does                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Avoid Notice      | Stealth while moving at half speed; you usually roll Stealth for initiative        |
| Defend            | Shield raised; you start the fight with Raise a Shield's benefits                  |
| Detect Magic      | Cast _detect magic_ at intervals as you go                                         |
| Follow the Expert | An expert ally guides you: add your level to the check, and +2 to +4 by their rank |
| Hustle            | Double travel speed for Constitution × 10 minutes (at least 10)                    |
| Investigate       | Secret Recall Knowledge checks about what you pass                                 |
| Repeat a Spell    | Keep casting a short spell, usually a cantrip                                      |
| Scout             | Everyone in the party gets +1 circumstance to their next initiative roll           |
| Search            | Seek for hidden doors, hazards and objects as you go                               |

_Player Core pp. 438–439._
