Any hero can use any gear, but their class says what they're good with. Inventory is counted in slots, and Armor only helps when you Defend.

## Proficiency

- **Weapons you aren't proficient with can't crit.**
- **Defending in armor you aren't proficient with costs 1 more action.**
- Swapping weapons or shields you're proficient with is **free once a round**.

_Core p. 32._

## Armor

Armor is what you subtract from a hit when you **Defend**. **Unarmored (or in ordinary clothes), your Armor equals your DEX.**

{{nimble-armor}}

"Req." is the STR the armor or shield expects. The book doesn't say what happens without it.

_Core pp. 32–33._

_Core pp. 34–35._

## Dual wielding, fists and improvised weapons

- **Dual wielding** two Light weapons lets you attack with **advantage once a round**. With STR 3+ you can dual wield one-handed weapons that aren't Light; with STR 2, one of the two can be non-Light. Two different weapons: roll both and pick either result.
- **Unarmed strike**: roll 1d4; on a hit, deal **1 + STR**. Fists and feet count as dual wielded.
- **Improvised weapons** deal 1d4+STR or 1d6+STR, and may break on a crit.

_Core p. 34._

## Inventory slots

You have **10 + STR** slots for everything you carry, held, worn or packed.

| Slots | Holds                                                                                |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | A one-handed weapon, a shield, worn armor, a stack of javelins, 500 gp, or 2 potions |
| 2     | A two-handed weapon, unworn armor, or anything similarly bulky                       |

Small related items can share a slot (camping supplies, for example).

_Core p. 20._

## Money

**10 silver (sp) = 1 gold (gp).** A humble meal is about 1 sp, a feast 1 gp. Small towns rarely have anything worth more than 50 gp; commissioning a special item starts around 50 gp.

_Core pp. 36–37._
