Heroes get **3 actions** on their turn. Most things cost 1 action; big spells and abilities cost more. Reactions use those same actions on other creatures' turns.

## Heroic actions

| Action     | Cost     | What it does                                                          |
| ---------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attack     | 1 action | Anything that can harm an enemy: a weapon, a harmful spell, a Grapple |
| Cast Spell | Varies   | Needs a free hand (or a held focus), your voice, and any mana         |
| Move       | 1 action | Up to your Speed (6); break it up around other actions if you like    |
| Assess     | 1 action | A DC 12 skill check to read the fight (below)                         |

_Core pp. 12–13._

## Assess

Pick one, then make a **DC 12** check with a skill that fits the moment (Might to make an opening up close, Arcana about an elemental):

- **Ask a Question** about an enemy's weakness, abilities or plans, the place or the story. The GM answers honestly.
- **Create an Opening**: the next Primary Die rolled against a target this round gets +1.
- **Anticipate Danger**: every Primary Die rolled against you this round gets –1.

You can't Assess with the same skill twice in one encounter.

_Core p. 13._

## Free actions

Free actions cost no action or mana unless they say so. Once a turn you can do a **simple task for free**: open an unlocked door, shout a phrase, drop an item, end Concentration. Swapping to a weapon or shield you're proficient with is free once a round.

_Core pp. 13, 32._

## Heroic reactions

Reactions are used when it isn't your turn. **Each costs 1 action**, so you start your next turn with fewer, and you can use **each reaction once per round**.

{{nimble-reactions}}

- You can **Interpose and Defend** on the same attack if you have the actions; then neither is available again until your next turn is over.
- Only heroes make Opportunity Attacks; monsters don't.
- Defend can't always stop everything (psychic damage, some areas). Defending in armor you aren't proficient with costs 1 more action.

_Core pp. 14, 32._

## Other common actions

| Action                 | Cost     | Notes                                               |
| ---------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Hide                   | 1 action | DC 15 Stealth from cover; automatic with full cover |
| Drink or give a potion | 1 action | To yourself or an adjacent willing creature         |
| Escape a grapple       | 1 action | A successful STR or DEX save                        |
| Stand up from Prone    | 3 spaces | Costs movement, not an action                       |
| Load a crossbow        | 1 action | 2 for a handheld ballista                           |

_Core pp. 10–11, 35–36._
