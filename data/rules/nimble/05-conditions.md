Attacks, traps and spells can leave a condition behind. Some last a round, some until something cures them, and some can be shaken off by spending an action on the right save.

## Same effect, other names

- **Paralyzed, Stunned and Unconscious** all mean **Incapacitated**: you can't do anything, attacks against you have advantage, and melee attacks that hit you crit.
- **Petrified** is Incapacitated too, and you're immune to most damage except big explosions, picks and the like.
- **Restrained** works like Grappled but comes from objects (chains, rope, roots) and ignores size.
- **Fighting in the dark** makes you Blinded.

_Core pp. 10–11, 36, 56._

## Minor statuses

**Smoldering**, **Charged**, **Distracted** and the like do nothing on their own and **end when combat does**. Certain spells and abilities do more against creatures that have them:

| Status     | Meaning                                                          |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Smoldering | Set by some fire spells; other fire spells hit it harder         |
| Charged    | Taking lightning damage charges you for 1 minute                 |
| Distracted | Adjacent to or Taunted by an ally of yours, or unable to see you |

_Core pp. 11, 46, 48, 56._
