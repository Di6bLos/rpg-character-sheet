HP come back quickly with Hit Dice and rest; Wounds are slow and mostly heal at a Safe Rest.

## Hit Dice

- Hit Dice are spent to regain HP. Their size comes from your class.
- You have **1 Hit Die at level 1**, and your maximum goes up by **1 every level**.
- They all come back on a **Safe Rest**.

_Core pp. 9, 21._

## Field rests

Out in the wild, you can rest to spend Hit Dice:

| Rest         | Time                                 | Each Hit Die you spend heals |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Catch Breath | 10 minutes or more                   | Its roll + STR               |
| Make Camp    | 8 hours or more, with food and sleep | Its maximum + STR            |

Spend dice one at a time, as many as you like. With a negative STR, subtract it from each die.

_Core p. 16._

## Safe rests

A Safe Rest happens somewhere truly safe that the GM agrees on: an inn overnight, a hidden oasis, a stocked cabin, a sacred shrine. **Camping in the wilderness or a dungeon isn't enough.**

After a Safe Rest you recover **all your HP, all your Hit Dice, your mana and other class resources**, and heal **1 Wound**. Temporary HP expire.

If your table wants a slower recovery, try a week of rest per Wound healed.

_Core pp. 9, 16._

## Temporary HP

Temp HP are lost before your normal HP. They **don't add together**: if you'd gain more, choose which amount to keep. They expire after a Safe Rest.

_Core p. 9._

## Potions

Drinking a potion, or giving one to an adjacent willing creature, takes **1 action**.

| Potion                 | Heals | Cost   |
| ---------------------- | ----- | ------ |
| Healing Potion         | 2d4+4 | 50 gp  |
| Greater Healing Potion | 3d6+6 | 150 gp |
| Supreme Healing Potion | 4d8+8 | 450 gp |

Small towns stock only a few.

_Core pp. 36–37._

## Downtime

Time between adventures. Much of it can be skipped, but heroes can **retrain** abilities (even a subclass, if the story fits), gather information, pursue personal goals, buy and sell, perform, craft, socialize, invest, mentor, research, serve a patron, or build something.

_Core p. 17._
