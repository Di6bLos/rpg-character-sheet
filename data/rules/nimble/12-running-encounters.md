For the GM: what monsters do by default, and how many to put in a fight.

## Monsters

- Monsters **don't use heroic actions or reactions**: they move, use what's on their stat block, and end their turn. They **die at 0 HP**.
- Unless the stat block says otherwise, a monster is **Medium**, **unarmored**, has **Speed 6**, attacks at **Reach 1**, and rolls a plain **d20 for saves**.
- A monster can swap an attack for another move, or either one for another try at a save.
- **Flunkies** are ordinary monsters that **can't crit**.
- Tell players when a monster has armor; a goblin with a shield has Medium Armor, a metal golem Heavy. Heavy Armor halves the dice, rounding up.

_GM's Guide p. 24._

## Building an encounter

Add up the heroes' levels and spend that many monster levels. A level 1/3 monster counts as a third of a level.

| Difficulty  | Monster levels vs. hero levels | Per typical session             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Easy        | Less than half                 | 1–2                             |
| Medium      | About 75%                      | 1–2                             |
| Hard        | Equal                          | 1                               |
| Deadly      | 100–125%                       | Sparingly                       |
| Very deadly | 150% or more                   | Only after a warned-off blunder |

- Aim for **1–4 monsters per hero**, minions aside. Use a legendary monster for a lone boss, minions for a horde.
- A session usually has **2–5 combat encounters**.
- For variety, roughly **60% unarmored, 30% Medium, 10% Heavy** armor.
- Start easy: it's simpler to make a fight harder mid-way than easier.

_GM's Guide pp. 24–26._

## Minions

Adding minions in waves is an easy way to tune a fight: **1 per hero** is slightly harder, **2–3** noticeably, **4** much more. Any damage kills one; optionally, use their die size to let overkill damage spill over.

| Party level | Minion die |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| 1–3         | d4         |
| 3–5         | d6         |
| 5–10        | d8         |
| 10–13       | d10        |
| 13–17       | d12        |
| 17–20       | d20        |

The bands overlap as printed.

_GM's Guide p. 26._

## Tuning in play

- **Focus fire** makes a fight harder; spreading damage or sending foes in waves makes it easier.
- Monsters that **move** a lot force heroes to reposition and open up Opportunity Attacks.
- Moving monsters **earlier in the round** makes the fight harder.
- When in doubt, **rule in favour of the heroes**.

_GM's Guide pp. 3, 26._
