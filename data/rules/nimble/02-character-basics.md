Making a hero: pick a class, then an ancestry and background, then fill in the sheet. You can do the steps in any order.

## Making a hero

1. **Class.** It shapes everything else. The Core Rules list Berserker, the Cheat, Commander, Hunter, Mage, Oathsworn, Shadowmancer, Shepherd, Songweaver, Stormshifter and Zephyr; the classes themselves are in the Heroes book.
2. **Ancestry and background**, and optionally what drives your hero to adventure.
3. **Stats**: choose an array and put the highest numbers in your class's Key Stats. Mark your advantaged (+) and disadvantaged (–) saves.

| Array    | Values         |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Standard | +2, +2, +0, –1 |
| Balanced | +2, +1, +1, +0 |
| Min–Max  | +3, +1, –1, –1 |

_Core pp. 18–20._

## Skills

Each skill starts at its stat. Then place **4 more skill points** wherever you like (all in one skill, spread out, anything).

| Skill       | Stat | Covers                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arcana      | INT  | Magic and magic items; Aberrations, Elementals, Oozes                                     |
| Examination | INT  | Clues, injuries, traps and mechanisms; Constructs                                         |
| Finesse     | DEX  | Locks, traps, sleight of hand, piloting, careful climbing                                 |
| Influence   | WIL  | Persuading, deceiving, negotiating, performing                                            |
| Insight     | WIL  | Motives, lies, emotions; even "my hero would have thought of that"                        |
| Lore        | INT  | History, kingdoms, religions; Celestials, Dragons, Fey, Fiends, Giants, Humanoids, Undead |
| Might       | STR  | Lifting, breaking, climbing, swimming, jumping                                            |
| Naturecraft | WIL  | Survival, tracking, animals; Beasts, Monstrosities, Plants                                |
| Perception  | WIL  | Spotting hidden things and noticing you're watched                                        |
| Stealth     | DEX  | Hiding and moving quietly                                                                 |

_Core pp. 6–8, 20._

## Secondary numbers

| On the sheet            | Default                              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Max HP and Hit Die size | From your class                      |
| Hit Dice                | Equal to your level                  |
| Initiative              | DEX                                  |
| Speed                   | 6                                    |
| Max Wounds              | 6                                    |
| Inventory slots         | 10 + STR                             |
| Armor                   | DEX unarmored, or your armor's value |

_Core pp. 20, 32._

## Equipment and languages

- Start with your class's and background's **starting gear, or 50 gp** to buy your own. Starting above level 1, multiply that gold by your level.
- No need to track ammunition: a quiver means enough arrows.
- Everyone speaks **Common**, plus **one more language per point of INT**.

_Core p. 20._
