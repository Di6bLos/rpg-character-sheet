The GM decides when heroes level up, usually after finishing a quest or adventure that challenged them. Heroes go from level 1 to 20.

## When you gain a level

- **HP**: roll your Hit Die **with advantage** and add it to your max HP.
- **Hit Dice**: your maximum goes up by 1 (it normally equals your level).
- **Skills**: gain **1 skill point**, and you may move 1 point from one skill to another (no skill can go negative, or above +12).
- **Class features** for the new level: mana, spells, subclass features and so on.
- **Knock-on changes**: if a stat went up, update what depends on it (skills, damage, Initiative, Armor, mana, languages).

_Core p. 21._

## What the classes gain

Every class in the Heroes book follows the same rhythm:

| Level        | Gain                                          |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 3, 7, 11, 15 | Subclass features (you choose it at 3)        |
| 4, 8, 12, 16 | +1 to a Key Stat                              |
| 5, 9, 13, 17 | +1 to a Secondary Stat                        |
| 19           | An Epic Boon (from the GM's Guide)            |
| 20           | Capstone ability, including +1 to any 2 stats |

Spellcasters unlock tier 1 spells early (level 2 for the Mage) and a new tier about every other level after that; the Mage's cantrips grow stronger at 5, 10, 15 and 20.

_Heroes pp. 32–33 (the Mage's table) and each class's level table._

## Optional: multiclassing

When you level up you can take a level in any class, gaining its level 1 features instead of your current class's next level. You keep every class's equipment proficiencies, but use the saves of the class you have the most levels in. The GM may veto overpowered combinations.

_Core p. 54._
