Nimble keeps the dice simple: a d20 plus a skill or stat for checks and saves, and extra dice for advantage and disadvantage.

## Stats and KEY

Heroes have four stats: two **Key Stats** for their class and two **Secondary Stats**. A stat normally tops out at **+5**.

| Stat               | Used for                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strength (STR)     | STR weapon damage, Wounds resistance, HP recovery, Concentration, carrying, Grappling, Might |
| Dexterity (DEX)    | DEX weapon damage, Initiative, Armor, Grappling, Stealth, Finesse                            |
| Intelligence (INT) | Languages, spellcasting, wands and scrolls, Arcana, Examination, Lore                        |
| Will (WIL)         | Spellcasting, Insight, Influence, Naturecraft, Perception                                    |

- **KEY** in an ability or spell means either of your Key Stats.
- A stat written before a die means that many dice: "WIL d8" with WIL 2 is 2d8.
- **LVL** means your level.

_Core pp. 6–7, 56._

## Skill checks

When you try to affect the world, the GM may call for a check: **d20 + your skill**. Meet or beat the DC to succeed.

- A skill's bonus can never go above **+12**.
- **A natural 1 always fails; a natural 20 always succeeds.**

| Difficulty          | DC  |
| ------------------- | --- |
| Easy                | 8   |
| Medium              | 12  |
| Challenging         | 15  |
| Very difficult      | 18  |
| Extremely difficult | 20+ |

_Core p. 8._

## Saves

When the world affects you, roll a save: **d20 + the stat** (STR, DEX, INT or WIL). A 1 always fails and a 20 always saves, and you can always **choose to fail** instead of rolling.

- Every hero has **one advantaged save, one disadvantaged save and two neutral ones**, set by class. A Berserker rolls STR saves with advantage and INT saves with disadvantage.
- **The DC of effects a hero causes is 10 + KEY**, unless the effect says otherwise.

| Save | Tests                                                                 |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| STR  | Fitness: forced movement, restraint, poison, extreme temperatures     |
| DEX  | Agility and speed: diving clear of an explosion, keeping your footing |
| INT  | Seeing through tricks and illusions                                   |
| WIL  | Courage and personality: resisting charm and fear                     |

_Core p. 8._

## Advantage and disadvantage

- **Advantage**: roll one more die of the same type and drop the lowest. **Disadvantage**: roll one more and drop the highest.
- They stack: each instance adds a die (advantage 2 rolls two extra dice and drops the two lowest).
- Each advantage **cancels** one disadvantage before you roll.
- On ties, drop dice **from left to right**.
- It works on any roll, damage included: a 2d6 greataxe with advantage rolls 3d6 and drops the lowest.

_Core pp. 8, 56._

## Concentration

You can concentrate on **one thing at a time**. If you're **crit while concentrating**, make a **DC 10 STR save** or lose it. Concentration also breaks whenever you drop to 0 HP or are incapacitated. Ending it is a free action.

_Core pp. 10, 13._
