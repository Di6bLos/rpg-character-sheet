Movement is counted in spaces: each square or hex is about 5 feet. Close enough is good enough.

## Speed

- Speed is **6** unless something says otherwise. You can move horizontally or diagonally, and split your movement around other actions.
- Each extra **Move** action moves you your Speed again.
- You can pass through **allies' spaces**, and through **enemies' spaces as difficult terrain**, but can't end your move in an occupied space.
- **Difficult terrain halves your speed.** Climbable surfaces (trees, cliffs, some buildings) count as difficult terrain.
- **Prone**: movement costs double, and standing up costs **3 spaces**.

_Core pp. 10–12._

## Falling and forced movement

- **Falling**: **1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet** (2 spaces).
- **Pushed into an obstacle**: 1d6 bludgeoning for every space the movement was cut short. Hitting another creature splits that damage between you.
- **Knockback / Push X** moves a creature X spaces and ignores difficult terrain.
- **Teleporting** doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

_Core pp. 10, 56._

## Size

| Size       | Guide                                   |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Tiny       | Fits in a pocket; several share 1 space |
| Small      | Fits in a backpack; 2 share 1 space     |
| Medium     | A human; 1 per space                    |
| Large      | A bear; a 2×2 area                      |
| Huge       | A small house; a 3×3 area               |
| Gargantuan | A castle keep; 4×4 or more              |

_Core p. 8._

## Areas

- **Cone X**: from you, out X spaces and up to X spaces wide at the far end.
- **Line X**: up to X spaces in one direction, 1 space wide.
- **Reach X**: everything within X spaces of you.
- A space at least half covered by the cone or line is in it.
- **Diagonals** count as adjacent by default; the table can count every other diagonal instead.

_Core pp. 55–56._

## Optional: abstract distances

Without a grid, use **Close**, **Midrange** and **Far**. Close creatures are within Reach/Range 4, Midrange within 6, and Far is beyond that. One move from Midrange gets you to Close or Far.

_Core p. 10._

## Light and darkness

- Heroes can't normally see in the dark, and **fighting in the dark makes you Blinded**.
- **Torches and lanterns** light up to **6 spaces**. A torch lasts about one dungeon (or one level of a big one); a lantern with oil lasts until your next Safe Rest.
- Light, fire, lightning and radiant flashes can draw attention.
- **Darkvision X** sees normally in the dark out to X spaces; **Blindsight X** senses everything within X spaces regardless.

_Core pp. 36, 56._
