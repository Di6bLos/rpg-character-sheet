Spells come from schools. Tiered spells cost mana equal to their tier; cantrips are free and grow stronger as you level.

## Schools and tiers

There are **six schools**: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Radiant and Necrotic. Each has its **cantrips** and **9 tiers** of stronger spells, unlocked as your class levels up. You can cast **any spell from a school you know, up to the tier you've unlocked**.

| School    | Style                                                                              |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fire      | High, steady damage at medium range; some set targets Smoldering                   |
| Ice       | Medium damage at long range, control and protection; more against Hampered targets |
| Lightning | High damage at long range and teleporting; some can strike the caster              |
| Wind      | Moving friend and foe, cutting through groups, extra damage on crits               |
| Radiant   | Punishing the wicked, protecting, healing, even raising the dead                   |
| Necrotic  | Minions, traps and manipulation; risky spells that may hit friends too             |

Each school also has **utility spells**, non-combat cantrips that some classes learn as they level.

_Core pp. 44–45, 52–53._

## Mana

- **A spell's mana cost equals its tier. Cantrips cost no mana.**
- **Upcasting**: spend extra mana for a spell's upcast effect, once per extra mana, but never past the tier you've unlocked.
- Your class sets your mana pool. For example, the Mage's is **(INT × 3) + LVL** and the Oathsworn's **WIL + LVL**; both refill on a Safe Rest.

_Core pp. 12, 44; Heroes pp. 32, 38._

## Casting

- You need **one free hand** (or a held spellcasting focus) and **your voice**.
- **Area and multi-target spells don't miss or crit**, and one roll applies to all targets. Conditions on individual targets (Blinded, Grappled) don't change that roll; your own advantage and disadvantage still do.
- **Save spells** use your save DC, usually **10 + KEY**.
- **Range** spells have disadvantage if an enemy is adjacent, and can take 1 disadvantage for +2 Range (up to +6). **Reach** spells can't stretch but work normally in melee.
- A harmful spell is an attack, so it counts toward rushed attacks.
- Many spells need **Concentration**: one at a time, a DC 10 STR save when you're crit, and it breaks at 0 HP.

_Core pp. 10, 12–13, 44._

## Special dice

**d44, d66 and d88**: roll two dice of that size, the left one is the tens and the right one the ones. These rolls **can't miss or crit**. With advantage, roll three and drop the lowest.

_Core p. 56._

## Scrolls and wands

- A **spell scroll** is single use. Reading it takes the spell's normal actions and **no mana**, and anyone who can read its language can try. If you don't know a spell from that school, it takes a **DC 10 Arcana** check, and a failure wastes it.
- A **wand** also casts without mana and can be recharged. Heroes **proficient with wands** use any school's wand normally; anyone else needs a **DC 10 + spell tier Arcana** check, and the charge is spent either way.

_Core p. 42._
