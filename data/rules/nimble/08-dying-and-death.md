HP measure how much a hero can shrug off in a fight. Wounds measure how close they are to death.

## Dropping to 0 HP

HP can't go below 0. When you drop to **0 HP** you gain **1 Wound** and the **Dying** condition, which lasts until you regain any HP. While Dying:

- you have **only 1 action**, and your Concentration breaks;
- **attacking or casting** gives you **1 Wound** unless you make a **DC 10 STR save**;
- **taking damage** gives you **2 Wounds**, or **3** from a crit.

_Core pp. 8–9, 11._

## Wounds and death

- Wounds are serious injuries. HP come back fast; Wounds usually heal **1 per Safe Rest**.
- **At 6 Wounds you die**, unless an ability changes that number.
- Any Wounds at all give you the **Wounded** condition.
- Bringing a hero back from death is possible but rare, and usually comes at a steep price.

_Core pp. 8–9, 11._

## Bloodied

At **half your HP or less** you're **Bloodied**. It does nothing alone, but some abilities care.

_Core p. 11._

## Optional: gritty dying

For a deadlier game, the GM can lower the Wound limit (5 is a bit harder, 1–2 very lethal), or give each Wound a cumulative –1 to skill checks, saves, or all rolls.

_Core p. 9._

## Optional: other variants

- **Fast Resting**: a Safe Rest heals every Wound.
- **Playing Dead**: at 0 HP a hero can fall prone and try an Influence check to play dead.
- **Retreat**: on their turn any hero can call for a retreat; if the party agrees, the GM lets them flee, possibly at a cost.

_Core p. 54._
